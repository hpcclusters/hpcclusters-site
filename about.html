Meet the HPC Team

HPCClusters is a small, independent practice — not a reseller. Our engineers and executives bring a combined well over a century of experience in HPC, cloud, and data centers.

SJ

Steve Jones, Founder

Steve Jones is currently the Director of the High Performance Computing Center at Stanford University. In this role, he oversees the management and operation of a large-scale computing environment that primarily supports federally-funded sponsored research.

Jones is also active in the broader HPC and AI industry community, helping convene leaders from academia, government, and industry to advance how these technologies are applied to some of the world's most pressing challenges.

He has designed and administered numerous Top 500 supercomputers, speaks regularly about the management of high performance computing clusters, and offers a number of graduate-level, continuing education, and HPC training courses.

Jones is passionate about the small-to-medium HPC cluster enterprise and enabling others in the space to leverage well-established free and open-source solutions to support their environments.

  • Director, High Performance Computing Center — Stanford University
  • Active in the international HPC and AI industry community
  • Designer & administrator of numerous Top 500 supercomputers
  • Advises on AI/HPC infrastructure strategy — workload sizing, deployment models, and data center facility design
  • Instructor, graduate-level & continuing-education HPC courses
