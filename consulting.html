Ways to engage

Four ways we work with clients

Pick a starting point — you can move between them as your infrastructure and team mature.

AI & HPC Infrastructure Strategy

Quite often clients simply need a consultant with no financial motivation to offer guidance. We help classify your AI and HPC workload mix — training, inference, agentic, simulation — balance CPU against GPU capacity, and turn that into a clear build/buy/colocate/cloud roadmap. We use OpenHPC and Kubernetes ourselves and support the same.

HPC & Kubernetes Cluster Engineering

Do you need assistance getting up and running with a modern cluster? We work with you through base and extended OpenHPC installation, and deploy Kubernetes alongside it — so traditional batch HPC and containerized AI/ML workloads run on shared infrastructure instead of two disconnected silos.

Systems Administration & Operations

We function as an extension of your operations team — deployment strategy, capacity planning and utilization, refresh cycles, and ongoing software installation and troubleshooting across on-prem, hybrid, and cloud environments.

Data Center & Facility Design

Server and cluster architecture is only half the system. We advise on power density, cooling strategy (air, liquid, or hybrid), rack layout, and facility lifecycle — so the physical plant can actually support the AI and HPC workloads you're planning for.

How it works

A vendor-neutral path from strategy to steady-state

01 · Strategy

Classify the workload

We start with your workload mix, growth plans, and constraints, and map it to a build/buy/colocate/cloud decision — no pre-baked hardware or software recommendation.

02 · Engineer

Deploy OpenHPC + Kubernetes

We work alongside you through cluster installation — traditional HPC scheduling and cloud-native orchestration, integrated rather than bolted together.

03 · Operate

Run it at steady state

We extend your operations team on capacity planning, utilization, troubleshooting, and remote management over time.

04 · Facility

Design the space around it

Power, cooling, density, and layout — sized to the infrastructure you're actually running, not a generic template.
